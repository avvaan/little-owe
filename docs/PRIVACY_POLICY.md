# Privacy Policy — Little Owl

**Last updated: 14 September 2026**

Little Owl is a toy for children aged about three to six. This policy is short because
there is very little to say: **the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way
to.**

---

## The short version

- **No data is collected.** Not about your child, not about you, not about the device.
- **The app never connects to the internet.** There is no networking code in it at all.
- **No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **No third-party software** of any kind is built into the app.
- The only thing stored is **your own settings**, on your own iPad.

---

## The microphone

Your child talks to the owl, so the app asks for the microphone. It asks the first time
your child taps the owl, and never before that.

While your child is talking, the sound is held in the iPad's memory for a few seconds so
the owl can repeat it back in a funny voice. Then it is released.

- It is **never written to a file**, not even a temporary one.
- It is **never sent anywhere**. There is nowhere for it to be sent to.
- Nothing is kept after the owl has finished speaking.

If you do not allow the microphone, the app still works. The owl simply waits a moment
instead of listening.

## Speech recognition

In the prayers and rhymes, the word games and the "why?" questions, the owl needs to know
whether your child said something. That uses Apple's speech recognition, and the app
requires it to run **entirely on your iPad** — the setting for it is switched on in the
code and the app will not fall back to a version that uses a server.

What was recognised is used for one turn — to notice that your child spoke, or to pick
which pre-written answer the owl gives — and then it is gone. It is not stored, not shown
to anyone, and not sent anywhere.

If speech recognition is unavailable or you do not allow it, every one of those modes
still works. The owl waits a moment instead, and the games and questions offer cards to
tap.

## What the app stores

Exactly one thing: **the choices you make in the grown-up settings**, saved on your iPad
in the app's own storage.

That is four pieces of information:

1. which objects are in the room;
2. which prayers and rhymes are on the lamp;
3. whether captions are shown;
4. how loud the owl's voice is.

There is **no record of what your child said, asked, answered or played**, how long they
played, or when. None of that was ever collected, so there is none of it to delete.

Deleting the app removes those settings with it.

## Children's privacy

Little Owl is made for children and is listed in the App Store's Kids category.

No personal information is collected from anyone using this app, children included. The
app does not ask for a name, an age, an email address, a photograph or a location, and it
has no field in which any of those could be typed. Nothing your child does in the app
leaves your iPad.

The app contains no advertising, no links out to other websites or apps, no social
features, and nothing to buy. Everything a grown-up can change sits behind a gate your
child cannot pass.

## Sharing with others

Nothing is shared with anyone, because there is nothing to share and no one to share it
with. The app has no servers, no analytics provider, no advertising partner and no
third-party code.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before
that version is released, and the change will be described here rather than quietly made.
